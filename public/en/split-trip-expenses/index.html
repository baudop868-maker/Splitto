How to split travel expenses with friends

On a trip someone different pays every time: the hotel, the fuel, the dinners. Back home nobody remembers who owes what. Here is a simple method that keeps the peace.

1. Log every expense right away

Write the expense down when you pay it: who paid and who it was for. Waiting until the end of the trip is the surest way to forget half of them. With Splitto you just open the group link on your phone. Nobody has to install anything.

2. Not every expense is for everyone

If Luke skipped the excursion or Julia doesn't drink wine, that expense is split only among the people who were there. Three rules cover almost every case:

  • Equally among the people involved (dinners, fuel, hotel).
  • By shares when someone uses more: whoever has a single room pays, say, 2 shares instead of 1.
  • Exact amounts when everyone knows what they had.

3. Different currencies? Pick a group currency

Abroad, some costs will be in pounds or dollars. Convert everything into the group currency using the rate your bank or card actually charged.

4. Settle up with as few transfers as possible

Not everyone needs to pay everyone. Work out what each person paid against their share, then the people below zero pay the people above zero. Example with three friends and €480 of costs (€160 each):

  • Mark paid the hotel: €300
  • Julia paid for dinners: €120
  • Luke paid for fuel: €60
