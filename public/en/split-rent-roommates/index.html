How to split rent and bills with roommates

Living together is easier when the money rules are clear from day one. Here is a fair way to split household costs.

Rent: split it by room

When rooms differ, an equal split feels simple but breeds resentment. A common fix is to give each room a number of shares: the big room counts for more, the small ones for less.

Example: €1,200 rent with shares of 4, 3 and 3. That is 10 shares, so each share is worth €120.

  • Big room (4 shares): €480
  • Medium room (3 shares): €360
  • Medium room (3 shares): €360

In Splitto choose "Shares" and enter 4, 3 and 3. The maths is automatic.

Bills: split equally

Power, gas and internet are usually split equally, since everyone uses them. If one roommate works from home all day you might agree on a slightly higher share: decide it before the bill arrives, not after.

Shared groceries

Detergent, toilet paper, oil, coffee: log who buys what and split it equally. Personal items stay personal.

Settle up once a month

Pick a fixed day, for example when rent is due, and settle everything at once. Fewer transfers, fewer arguments.
